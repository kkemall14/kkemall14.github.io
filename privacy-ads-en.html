Seni Dinliyorum — Privacy policy

New Android/iOS builds containing the advertising SDK • The previous store-release policy is preserved.

Scope and contact

This policy covers the new Android/iOS builds containing Google Mobile Ads SDK and the offline holiday bundle. Publishing this page does not mean a store update has shipped. The policy for older SDK-free releases remains on the support site. Test builds use test ads only; live advertising requires separate activation. Developer/controller: Kemal Mert. Privacy and support: Resect@windowslive.com. Updated: 5 November 2026.

Personal entries and accounts

No app account is required. Notes, tasks, routines, habits, people/birthdays, addresses, belongings/clothing, vehicle entries, payment reminders, preferences and optional photos are stored in the device’s app space. They are not sent to a developer personal-record collection server or sold. User-selected exports/sharing and system services are described below.

Passwords and local protection

Secure password-section and PIN-related records use Flutter Secure Storage through Android secure storage or iOS Keychain. The optional PIN controls access to the screen; it does not mean every ordinary note is separately encrypted. Migration from older versions and system behavior may differ. You do not have to enter especially sensitive information.

Microphone, photos, calendar and notifications

Voice input uses the microphone and system speech recognition. Depending on device, language and settings, Apple, Google or the selected speech provider may process audio. We do not claim all speech processing is offline. The app does not keep audio recording files or send them to a developer server; you can type instead. Camera/photo access is for optional photos; notifications are for reminders. Calendar events are displayed read-only with permission: the app does not create, change or delete system calendar events. People entries are not an automatic phone-contact import. Manage permissions in system settings.

Advertising and data processed by Google

Advertising-enabled builds use a Google AdMob banner only on the Memory category overview. The app requests non-personalized ads; this does not mean no data is processed. Google may process IP addresses and inferred approximate location, device/app identifiers, advertising and app interactions, performance and crash information for ad delivery/measurement, analytics, diagnostics and abuse prevention. Processing depends on the OS, SDK version and privacy choices. Google SDK data is protected in transit with TLS. The app does not attach precise location or the contents of notes, tasks, passwords, photos, calendars, voice input or searches to ad requests, and does not send custom advertising user IDs. This version does not request ATT/IDFA authorization; this does not mean no other app/device identifiers are processed.

Advertising choices and your rights

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents advertising privacy choices where applicable to your region. You can accept, refuse or manage the choices presented. Revisit applicable options at Settings → Ad privacy; available options depend on your region. The app makes no new ad request when advertising eligibility cannot be verified. Existing banners are removed while choices change. Refusing ads or limiting an advertising ID does not necessarily stop all technical processing. Google’s data/retention information and privacy controls are linked below. The developer cannot directly delete data held by Google. Send requests concerning access, correction or deletion of developer-held support correspondence to the contact address; applicable regional rights remain available.

Holidays, widgets and sharing

The new build reads holiday dates offline from the app bundle; country, region and year are not sent to a holiday service. Coverage for 2026–2030 is limited to 207 countries and 366 source first-level subdivisions, not every local observance. Calendar changes require app/data updates. The independent data archive and sources/licenses are linked below and accessible from the app’s holiday country/region setting. Widget snapshots stay in the device app space, using an App Group on iOS. Incoming shared text does not create tasks without preview and confirmation; queued shares older than 24 hours are removed when the queue is read. Content you choose to share out is received by the recipient/provider you select.

Backups, retention and deletion

Optional .sdy exports are encrypted with your chosen password. The local/cloud provider selected in the file picker may retain the file. The developer does not receive the export or password and cannot recover a forgotten password. Delete entries within the app and independently delete exported copies from their saved locations. Automatic Android app backup is disabled in the app manifest; device manufacturers, transfer tools and user-selected providers have their own behavior. iOS device/iCloud backups depend on system settings; Keychain records may survive uninstalling. We do not promise that data can never leave the device. Google’s policies govern retention of advertising data it processes.

Support, children and this site

If you email support, your email address and submitted content are processed by the mail service to answer your request. Do not send PINs, passwords or backup files. The app is not specifically directed at children. Personal numeric/habit logs are not medical diagnosis or treatment; there is no HealthKit integration. This static website cannot access app records and contains no forms, advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages may process connection information under GitHub’s own terms. Changes to this policy are dated on this page.
